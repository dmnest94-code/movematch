Privacy notice

Last updated: November 24, 2026. This draft reflects the inquiry form in this website package. The site owner should review it against actual business practices before publishing.

Information we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect the contact information you enter (name, phone, email) and details of your planned move, including ZIP codes, move date, preferred services and any optional notes. For office moves, we also ask for the address where an on-site walkthrough is requested. We may receive a basic source/campaign label when you came from a printed QR code.

How information is used

We use your information to review your request, respond, discuss options and, with your knowledge, help coordinate the next steps. Submitting this form does not book a moving service, create a moving contract or provide a binding estimate.

Storage and processing

This website uses Netlify Forms as its form-processing service. Submitted information may be stored and processed by Netlify. We do not request payment card information in the inquiry form.

Information sharing

Information may be shared with a potential moving service provider when needed to discuss a requested service and with your knowledge. We do not publish inquiry details as testimonials or advertisements without permission. The site owner should verify this paragraph matches actual processes before launch.

Questions and changes

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, contact the site operator using their contact details on the main website.
